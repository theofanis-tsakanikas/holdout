was a correct output of a ledger nobody had priced: the ERP's history was exported once per slice
on its last day, so no earlier sale had a cost. Exported daily, the coefficient of variation went
from 3.57 to 0.12 and the experiment exists. Eight branches from that review landed in a day;
the cycle that followed read out **+12,398 cents [+11,223, +13,571], p = 0.001** for the sized
experiment and refused the peeking one, and `destroy all` left the account clean. Also: the
runner reads `result_state`; the readout appends and restates and runs the compiled pinned query;
